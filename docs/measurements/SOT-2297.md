# SOT-2297 — Animal husbandry (GOOSE/COW/SHEEP) into exclusive EGG/MILK/WOOL sinks (PROMOTED)

## Axis / hypothesis

The champion (`main.py`, SOT-2260 + SOT-2298) produces **only crops** and never uses
`ANIMALS`. End-of-game self-mirror diagnosis shows MILK / WOOL / EGG market inventory
**below** `I0 = 10000` (unmet demand) with **zero supply** from either player — MILK
clears ≈ $272/unit, WOOL ≈ $209/unit at that scarcity. The prior land-expansion cycle
(SOT-2299) was competitively **dominated**: expanding into the shared MELON/STRAWBERRY
sink loses head-to-head because the non-expanding champion free-rides the same sink
(tragedy of the commons). Its recorded conclusion was explicit: *retry only with a sink
the champion supplies zero of, whose revenue beats its labor cost* — i.e. animals.
`EGG`/`MILK`/`WOOL` come **only** from `GOOSE→EGG`, `COW→MILK`, `SHEEP→WOOL`, so they
are an **exclusive** sink the crop-only champion cannot free-ride.

**Hypothesis:** convert a few of the low-value WHEAT tiles (preserve the proven MELON 10
/ STRAWBERRY 8 allocation) to COOP/PASTURE structures, run a small dedicated ranch crew,
and sell the resulting MILK/WOOL into the exclusive scarcity premium for a net final-money
gain after animal + wheat-feed + labor cost.

## Candidate (shipped)

Additive layer on the unchanged crop engine:

- **Tiles.** The `ANIMAL_PLAN = [("COW", 2), ("SHEEP", 2)]` structures take the 4 NW
  tiles nearest the shed (the spawn tile itself stays a clean pickup slot); the crop
  cluster is the remaining 21 tiles and keeps the MELON 10 / STRAWBERRY 8 / rest-WHEAT
  portfolio. `TARGET_HANDS` raised 5→6 to cover the extra chores.
- **Ranch crew.** The last `N_RANCHERS = 2` workers are dedicated ranchers (rest patrol
  crops). Each turn a rancher reactively: BUILD_PASTURE on an empty designated tile →
  PICKUP a bought animal at the shed → PLACE it → each day FEED (1 WHEAT from its own
  inv, drawn from the shed), CARE (fed-day-only yield bonus), HARVEST product into the
  shed. A rancher with no reachable chore falls through to the crop passes.
- **Market.** BUY_ANIMAL tops the plan up early (keeps a $500 cash buffer, per-type);
  a wheat feed **reserve** (`len(animals) · 3` = 12 units) is held back from selling and
  topped up via BUY_PRODUCT so animals never starve; the existing SOT-2298 metered,
  highest-price-first seller then clears MILK/WOOL/crops.
- Env animal constants mirrored exactly: `first_yield_day`/`interval`/`max_held`, wheat
  feed, and that CARE bonus is consumed only on a **fed** production day. Only `math` is
  imported (Kaggle exec harness: no `__file__`, no cwd use).
