## Interpretation — moon-449's league dominance is the exact artifact SOT-2417 proved inverts real LB
- moon-449 is **massively league-field-superior** to the champion (93.8% vs 34.4%, **+59.4 pp**), and it beats

- **But that basis is unreliable — proven by SOT-2417.** The tell is `roman`: moon-449 **crushes the weakest

  fragility signature** the real LB punished in soil (→ 600). Its 93.8% is therefore **NOT evidence of real-LB
  superiority over moon-198's 2543.4**. `CALIBRATED = False` reconfirmed (LB-anchored pairs checked: 0 — the
  candidate's LB is unknown, so the league still cannot certify it).
- Note also SOT-2383's own head-to-head history: moon-449 vs moon-198 was **MIXED 17W/3L (+2.7 %)**, below the
  lineage's 20/20 ALL-WIN confirm bar — i.e. even the head-to-head margin is thin and seed-fragile once you
