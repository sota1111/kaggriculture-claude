# SOT-2259 — Multi-tile farmer routing engine

## Change

Replace the single-tile wheat loop with a **multi-tile routing engine**: the
farmer patrols the whole unlocked NW quadrant (10x10 board → 5x5 = 25 slots),
planting / watering / harvesting many wheat tiles in parallel. Produce is sold
from the shed every turn on the independent market channel (the end-of-day
auto-drop moves farmer inventory to the shed).

- Crop: `WHEAT` (kept identical to the incumbent so the improvement isolates
  routing throughput, not crop choice — crop portfolio is deferred to SOT-2260).
- Slots: all 25 NW tiles, serviced nearest-first from the spawn corner.
- Watering: age 0 (keep-alive) + ages 2,3,4 (yield window
  `[(max_yield_day+1)//2, max_yield_day]`) → harvest at age 4 with the full
  4-unit yield.
- Weed reclaim: `DIG` weeds in-cluster to free slots over the 30-day game.
- Market: `SELL` all shed wheat each turn; `BUY_SEED` to keep a seed buffer
  sized to open slots.

## Environment / oracle

`kaggle_environments` `kaggriculture` (boardSize 10, turnsPerDay 24,
episodeSteps 720 → 30 days, startingMoney 3000). Score = final money.
