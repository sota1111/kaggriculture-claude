# SOT-2344 — Retune animal mix + feed/rancher logistics (COW2/SHEEP2 → COW3/SHEEP3, PROMOTED)

## Axis / hypothesis

SOT-2297 shipped `ANIMAL_PLAN = COW2/SHEEP2` and recorded that larger plans (COW3/SHEEP3,
COW2/SHEEP2+GOOSE2) *collapsed* to ~300–420 self-mirror, attributed loosely to
"cash/feed/crop over-extension". The parent cycle asked: is that collapse a real demand
limit, or a **feed-logistics failure** that better feed/rancher coordination can remove —
letting a larger exclusive-sink (MILK/WOOL) animal plan net-increase?

## Root-cause of the prior collapse (diagnosed, not assumed)

Reproduced on the committed champion code (self-mirror seed 1):

| plan | final money | animals alive (end) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| COW2/SHEEP2 | 51804 | COW2 SHEEP2 |
| COW3/SHEEP3 | **546** | **none** |
| COW2/SHEEP2+GOOSE2 | **411** | **none** |

Every animal in the 6-animal plans was **dead by end** → the collapse is *starvation*, not
a demand limit. Scaling `N_RANCHERS` alone did **not** rescue it (COW3/SHEEP3 self-mirror
seed 1: nr=2→546, nr=3→313, nr=4→546, nr=5→546, all animals dead) — so ranchers were not
the bottleneck. Tracing the market pass: the old buyer purchased the **whole** plan in one
day-0 order (`money >= cost + 500`, per type). A 6-animal plan sinks ~$2.7k up front,
dropping cash below the wheat-feed-reserve buy threshold; the animals then miss ≥2
consecutive feed days and **escape** (env `_daily_refresh_animals`: `consecutive_unfed >= 2`
→ structure reverts, animal gone). Cash spent, product never produced → farm collapses.

## Candidate (shipped)

Market pass, **feed-cash-priority staged animal buyer** (`main.py`):
- Compute the wheat feed-reserve cash need first; buy animals **one at a time, interleaved
  across types**, only while `money - cost >= feed_reserve_cash + CASH_BUFFER(500)`.
- Remaining animals are bought on later days as crop revenue accrues (staging), so the
  wheat feed reserve is always affordable and no animal starves.
- `ANIMAL_PLAN = COW3/SHEEP3` (6 animals); `N_RANCHERS = 2`, `WHEAT_FEED_RESERVE_DAYS = 3`
  unchanged. The COW2/SHEEP2 config is **behavior-identical** under the new buyer (it never
  hit the old cash ceiling) — verified `diff = +0.0` vs champion, so the change is a pure
  extension, not a regression risk for the prior optimum.
