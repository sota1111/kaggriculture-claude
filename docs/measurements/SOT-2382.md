# SOT-2382 — seat-diverse dual-route robustness (REJECTED / non-promotion)

Axis: `seat_diverse_dual_route_robustness` · cycle 2 · parent SOT-2379
Champion under test: `main.py` sha256 `00e8ab59…` (soil-remembers-rain, SOT-2381), a single
seat-0 distilled Tran H Hoang tape.

## Hypothesis (from the Issue)
The champion is a **seat-0-fixed** tape (`"""Fixed public Tran H Hoang policy … seat 0."""`). In the
arena, when assigned seat 1 with a different opponent, the tape's premise supposedly shifts and causes
seat-sensitive losses. Port the core of `kaitofukami/2-592-4-public-lb-v16-lightweight-anti-meta`
(verified LB 2592.4) = **position-diverse two-route** (a full seat0 route and a full seat1 route,
selected from the observed seat) as an additive flag-gated layer (OFF == champion) to fix the
disadvantaged seat.

## Result: REJECTED (main.py unchanged, docs-only)
Two independent findings, either of which alone blocks the port:

### 1. The source notebook no longer exists and its approach was superseded
- `kaggle kernels pull kaitofukami/2-592-4-public-lb-v16-lightweight-anti-meta` → **404 Not Found**
  (the kernel was overwritten/renamed). The author's current kaggriculture kernels are
  `v15-top5-robust-market-gate` and `29-30-current-holdout-v17-learned-market-ranker`.
- v17's own embedded validation table ranks the predecessors:
  | agent | train wins | holdout wins | holdout_min |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | v13 | 0/30 | 2/30 | 85197 |
  | **v16 route** | **5/30** | **7/30** | **85780** |
  | v16 threshold gate | 6/30 | 4/30 | 86003 |
  | v17 learned ranker | 27/30 | 29/30 | 101014 |
  The "v16 route" (the exact thing this Issue asks to port) is the author's **weak, retired** variant;
  they replaced it with a *learned market ranker*, not more route diversity. Our current champion scores
  ~150k–190k against the same opponent pool in the official env — i.e. porting v16-route would be a
  **regression**, not a lift. No seat-1 route trajectory is available to extract from any live source
  (the champion itself is a single seat-0 human-episode distillation with no seat-1 counterpart).

### 2. The champion is empirically seat-invariant — there is no disadvantaged seat to fix
Measured the committed champion's own final money as **seat 0** (`env.run([champ, opp])`) vs **seat 1**
(`env.run([opp, champ])`) — 6 disjoint seeds × 5 opponents × both orders (60 games), official
`kaggle_environments` "kaggriculture". `seat_asym = money(seat0) − money(seat1)`.

Self-mirror (cleanest seat-symmetry probe, champion vs identical champion):

| seed | 101 | 202 | 303 | 404 | 505 | 606 | 707 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| seat_asym | +586 | +0.0 | +0.0 | +0.0 | +1 | −84 | −120 |

The self-mirror seat asymmetry is **sub-0.1% of a ~150k score and flips sign** across seeds (seat0 tiny-ahead
on 101/505, seat1 tiny-ahead on 606/707, exactly tied on 202/303/404). There is **no systematic
disadvantaged seat** — the seat-0 tape replays on seat 1 with an outcome equal to seat 0 within
turn-order tie-break noise.

Against real opponents the same holds — the win/lose verdict and margin are essentially identical in
both seats; representative rows:

| opp | seed | seat0 champ | seat1 champ | verdict s0 / s1 | seat_asym |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| moon   | 505 | 87406 | 87405 | LOSE / LOSE | +1 |
| moon   | 707 | 158496 | 158605 | LOSE / LOSE | −109 |
| kf_v17 | 202 | 115617 | 115617 | LOSE / LOSE | +0.0 |
| kf_v17 | 606 | 151823 | 152105 | WIN / LOSE | −282 |
| kf_v17 | 707 | 160212 | 160341 | LOSE / **WIN** | −129 |
| roman  | 202 | 185354 | 185354 | WIN / WIN | +0.0 |
| pilk   | 606 | 145812 | 135469 | WIN / WIN | +10343 |

Where the champion **loses** (vs `moon` on every seed; vs `kf_v17` on some seeds) it loses by the **same
margin in both seats** → an **opponent-strength** deficit, not a seat deficit. Where the win-flips
(`kf_v17` 606 vs 707), the *advantaged* seat itself flips by seed, i.e. it is turn-order noise, not a
fixable seat-1 weakness. The larger `pilk` asyms come from the opponent's own seat-reactive play, yet
the champion still **wins both seats** there.

## Conclusion
A "position-diverse two-route" layer can only help if a *specific* seat is systematically disadvantaged.
The champion already performs identically (within <0.1% flip-sign noise) on both seats, so the mechanism
has **no deficit to address** and even a hypothetically perfect seat-1 route would gain ~0. Combined with
the source kernel being deleted/retired-weak, the axis is rejected.

- `main.py` unchanged (sha256 `00e8ab59…` = champion). No flag/layer added: adding an inert wrapper to a
  byte-identical frozen frontier tape (whose sha/exec-compat *is* the champion's identity) is pure risk
  for zero measured benefit and violates additive/scope-strict intent.
- Not submitted to Kaggle (parent SOT-2379 owns the slot).

## Next axis (recommendation)
The residual losses are opponent-strength, not seat. The productive escalation is a **stronger base
policy**, not seat diversification — e.g. evaluate porting the *v17 learned market ranker* itself, or
finish Child3 (SOT-2383) replay-league re-anchoring so champion selection tracks the true frontier.
