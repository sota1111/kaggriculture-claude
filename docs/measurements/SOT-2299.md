# SOT-2299 — Unlock NE/SW/SE and scale labor into scarce crop sinks (NON-PROMOTED)

## Axis / hypothesis

The champion (`main.py`, SOT-2260) farms only the always-unlocked NW quadrant (25
tiles) and never calls `BUY_LAND`. End-of-game diagnosis shows STRAWBERRY / TOMATO /
CARROT / WHEAT market inventory still **below** `I0 = 10000` (unmet demand headroom),
so the hypothesis was: unlock NE→SW→SE (`LAND_PRICES = [1000, 2000, 4000]`), assign
the extra tiles to the still-scarce high-value crops (**never more MELON** — it is
the only glutted product), and scale hired hands + routing to the new tiles, for a
net increase in final money after land + labor costs.

## Candidate (sweep-only, not shipped)

`cand.py` generalized the NW-only routing engine to **all unlocked quadrants**
(cluster = every tile whose quadrant is in `unlocked_quadrants`, distance-ordered
from the central shed corner), added **progressive `BUY_LAND`** (buy quadrant *k*
once `money ≥ LAND_PRICES[k] + buffer`, hour 0, atomic order), kept the proven NW
portfolio (MELON 10 / STRAWBERRY 8 / rest WHEAT), assigned extra-quadrant tiles to a
scarce-crop mix (no melon), and scaled hands as `BASE_HANDS + HANDS_PER_QUAD ·
(#extra unlocked quadrants)`.

## Environment / oracle

`kaggle_environments` `kaggriculture` (boardSize 10 → 4×25-tile quadrants,
turnsPerDay 24, episodeSteps 720 → 30 days, startingMoney 3000, farmHandCostMult 1;
hire cost = fib(nth-hire-of-day), reset nightly → labor cost grows steeply). Score =
final money (land + hire spend already deducted in-game). Harness `scripts/eval.py`,
`env.run([A, B])`, A = player 0. **vs champion** = promotion gate; **self-mirror**
(both farmers expand → both flood the shared sinks) = honest glut stress test.

## Decision — NON-PROMOTE (`main.py` unchanged)

Promotion gate = **vs-champion sign-consistent ALL WIN + diff_min > 0** (the standard
kaggriculture gate; the LB is relatively scored). The candidate is **ALL LOSE across
all 15 seeds and three seed families** → gate not met → **not promoted; `main.py`
reverted (left unchanged)**.

**Refined finding (more precise than the a-priori "demand-limited" worry):** land
expansion is **not** demand-limited — strawberry/tomato/carrot demand has real
headroom and the honest self-mirror shows a consistent **+4–7k absolute gain** after
land + labor costs. The expansion fails for a different, structural reason:

1. **High-value expansion (strawberry) is competitively dominated.** Strawberry is
   the champion's own crop, so both sell into the *same shared* sink. When one player
   expands, the non-expanding opponent **free-rides**: it pays no land/hire cost yet
   its strawberry still clears, so it out-earns the expander head-to-head
   (tragedy-of-the-commons — total extraction rises but the individual investment is
   dominated). Champion self-mirror 35.9k → **champion-vs-expander 40k**: the champion
   does *better* against an expander than against itself.
2. **Low-value expansion (tomato/carrot, which the champion ignores) does not pay
   for itself.** Its marginal revenue is below the land + fibonacci-hire cost, so it
   loses **even in self-mirror** (TOM/CAR-heavy configs: 32–34k < 35.9k baseline).

Either lever fails the vs-champion gate. Recorded to **suppress retry**: do not
revisit "buy land + scale labor into scarce sinks" without a *new* mechanism that
captures value the champion cannot free-ride on (e.g. an exclusive high-value sink,
or animals — GOOSE/COW/SHEEP produce EGG/MILK/WOOL the current champion never
supplies) **and** whose marginal revenue exceeds the fibonacci labor cost.
