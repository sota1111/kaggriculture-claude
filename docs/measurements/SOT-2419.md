## Interpretation — league field% is UNTRUSTWORTHY here; v17's score is indistinguishable from a known noise-floor agent
- v17's league field% = **64.6% — byte-for-byte the same score soil got in SOT-2417 (64.6%)**, and soil's
  **real LB is 600 (noise floor)**. The league cannot tell v17 apart from a *known* real-LB-inferior agent.
- v17 exhibits the **same capture-or-bust fragility** that the league rewards and the real LB punishes: it
  **busts to the moon-449 frontier tape (12.5%, ALL-but-3 LOSE)**, exactly the failure mode by which the
  league floored the real-LB champion moon-198. Its wins over roman (+3724 vs champion's +0.0 tie) and its
  near-coin-flip vs soil are precisely the head-to-head market-capture signal SOT-2417 proved is
  orthogonal-to-inverted from the real relative-percentile LB.
- Therefore, per the SOT-2417 redefined gate, v17's league superiority is **NOT evidence of real-LB
  superiority over moon-198's 2543.4**. `CALIBRATED = False` (the league still does not reproduce the
  real-LB ordering; moon-198 anchor reconfirmed at 27.5%).

## Decision — NON-PROMOTION (portable, but real-LB gate unconfirmable in this child)
- v17 is **portable** (offline exec PASS), so it is not rejected as non-portable.
- The **only trustworthy promotion gate is real-LB confirmation** (SOT-2417), which requires a Kaggle
  submission this child is **forbidden** to perform (the slot belongs to parent SOT-2412).
- The league field% is **non-gating** and, worse, ranks v17 identically to a known real-LB noise-floor
  agent (soil) with the same bust-to-frontier profile. So there is **no reliable evidence** to promote.
- ⇒ **NON-PROMOTION.** `main.py` remains the LB-proven **moon-198** champion (`7c5a4a2a`, real LB 2543.4);
  **no revert needed** (the candidate was tested as `cand_v17.py`, `main.py` was never changed);
  `submission.tar.gz` **not rebuilt**; **no Kaggle submission.**
