# SOT-2342 — Fertilizer synergy: collect animal byproduct → spray STRAWBERRY (PROMOTED)

## Axis / hypothesis

Every surviving animal (the COW3/SHEEP3 ranch from SOT-2297/SOT-2344) emits **one free
`FERTILIZER` byproduct per day** — the env sets `fertilizer_available = True` for each fed
animal in `_daily_refresh_animals` (kaggriculture.py L800). It was previously wasted. The
hypothesis: `COLLECT_FERTILIZER` it and `FERTILIZE` high-value crops to raise their yield,
for a net head-to-head gain at *zero capital cost* (only marginal rancher labor).

## Env mechanics (verified before implementing)

- `FERTILIZE` on a PLANT tile consumes 1 FERTILIZER and sets
  `fertilized_until_day = day + 2` → active for `day, day+1, day+2` (env L417-423).
- The bonus is realized **only on a watered production day**: yield accrues
  `+2 if (was_watered and fertilized_until_day >= day) else +1` (env L384 for non-ongoing
  WATER, L768-769 for ongoing daily refresh).
- **STRAWBERRY** (ongoing, `interval 2`, `max_yield 4`, `first_yield_day 10`) harvests each
  event as soon as yield accrues, so each of its four production events (ages 10/12/14/16)
  goes from **1 → 2 units** when fertilized+watered ⇒ strawberry output ≈ **doubles**.
- **MELON is excluded — fertilizing it is pure waste.** Non-ongoing (`max_yield 6`,
  `max_yield_day 12`); it accrues on WATER over the age 6–12 window (7 daily waters ≥ cap 6),
  so it *already* hits its 6-unit cap from watering alone and is harvested at age ≥ 12
  regardless. Fertilizer only makes it reach the cap a day or two sooner — zero extra units.
  Confirmed by the mechanic; strawberry is the only crop whose *harvested* total moves.

## Candidate (shipped)

Additive change in the **ranch pass only** (crop engine, market, portfolio, animal plan all
unchanged), guarded by `FERTILIZE_ENABLED` (off ⇒ byte-behaviour-identical to the champion):

1. **Collect** — a rancher standing on its animal tile does `COLLECT_FERTILIZER` as the
   *lowest* animal-chore priority (below FEED → HARVEST → CARE), and only while there is
   unfertilized strawberry demand and it holds `< FERT_CARRY_CAP (3)`. Animals are therefore
   never starved for fertilizer.
2. **Deliver** — carrying fertilizer with no pending animal survival chore, the rancher
   couriers to the nearest in-window STRAWBERRY (`9 ≤ age ≤ 16`, `fertilized_until_day < day`)
   and `FERTILIZE`s it. Routing does **not** claim the tile (only the in-place action does),
   so it never blocks a crop worker from watering that strawberry.
3. If not carrying and demand exists, route to an animal with a waiting byproduct.

Everything sits strictly below the proven animal chores, so the feed chain (all 6 animals
survive — SOT-2344) is preserved.

## Why this wins where land expansion (SOT-2299/SOT-2343) lost

Both add supply to a **shared** sink (strawberry), which normally invites free-riding
(tragedy of the commons). The difference is **cost**: land expansion paid land ($1000+) +
animals + a 3rd rancher + cross-board travel, so its marginal revenue lost to the
non-expanding champion that captured the freed crop sink. Here the fertilizer is a **free
byproduct** (zero capital cost) and the labor is marginal (ranchers have late-game slack once
the ranch is established), while the doubled crop is high-value STRAWBERRY. The extra
strawberry revenue beats the shared-sink price depression — and it shows up as a genuine
**absolute** gain, not just a head-to-head redistribution (self-mirror below).
