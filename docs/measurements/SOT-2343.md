# SOT-2343 — BUY_LAND new quadrants + additional EXCLUSIVE-sink animals (NON-PROMOTED)

## Axis / hypothesis

The champion (`main.py`, SOT-2344) farms only the always-unlocked NW quadrant (25
tiles): MELON 10 / STRAWBERRY 8 crops + **6 animals** (COW3 / SHEEP3) selling MILK /
WOOL into the exclusive scarcity sinks the crop-only opponent supplies zero of. It
never calls `BUY_LAND`.

SOT-2299 rejected land expansion into **shared crop** sinks (tragedy-of-commons: the
non-expanding champion free-rides the same MELON/STRAWBERRY market). Its explicit
recommended retry: *expand only into an **exclusive** sink the champion cannot
free-ride — animals (EGG/MILK/WOOL)*. This cycle tests that recommendation:
unlock NE→SW→SE (`LAND_PRICES = [1000, 2000, 4000]`) and place **additional
PASTURE/COOP animals** in the new quadrants, scaling ranchers + wheat feed reserve,
for a net final-money increase after land + animal + labor cost — **vs-champion sign
gate** (self-mirror absolute gain alone does not promote, per SOT-2299).

## Candidate (sweep-only, not shipped)

`cand_land.py` generalized the NW-only engine to **all unlocked quadrants**: per-quadrant
tile clusters distance-ordered from each quadrant's shed-access inner corner
(`NW=(4,4) NE=(5,4) SW=(4,5) SE=(5,5)`, all four are shed-adjacent so feed pickup works
from any unlocked corner); staged atomic `BUY_LAND` (unlock the next needed quadrant in
`LAND_ORDER` only when `money − land_cost ≥ feed_reserve_cash + buffer + next-animal`);
base NW COW3/SHEEP3 preserved; expansion animals placed near the new quadrant's corner;
crops kept NW-only (expansion is animal-priority, idle expansion tiles left empty so crop
workers are not dragged across the board); ranchers scaled via `N_RANCHERS`; wheat feed
reserve **ramped with animals actually owned** (`min(assigned_tiles, owned+N_RANCHERS)·3`
days) — a fixed `assigned_tiles·3` reserve counts not-yet-built structures, inflates
`feed_cash`, and starves the staged buyer so even the base NW animals are never bought
(6-animal-plan collapse to ~$650, all types identical). With no expansion the candidate is
**behavior-identical to `main.py`** (self-mirror diff +0.0).

## Environment / oracle

`kaggle_environments` `kaggriculture` (boardSize 10 → 4×25-tile quadrants; NW always
unlocked, others via `BUY_LAND` in fixed order NE/SW/SE at 1000/2000/4000; turnsPerDay 24,
episodeSteps 720 → 30 days, startingMoney 3000; hire cost = fib(nth-hire-of-day) reset
nightly). Score = final money (land + animal + hire spend already deducted in-game).
Harness `scripts/eval.py`, `env.run([A, B])`, A = player 0. **vs champion** = promotion
gate; **self-mirror** (both farmers expand) = honest absolute-gain stress test.

## Self-mirror — the absolute gain is genuine (like SOT-2299)

Config NE COW2/SHEEP2, NR3, self-mirror seeds 1–3: **61,147** vs champion self-mirror
**52,762** = **+8,385 / player**, symmetric (diff +0.0). So — exactly as in SOT-2299 —
the expansion produces a **real absolute gain** in a symmetric world; the exclusive
MILK/WOOL sinks are *not* saturated symmetrically. It is nonetheless **competitively
dominated head-to-head**.

## Mechanism — exclusivity of the sink does NOT rescue land expansion

SOT-2299's recommendation assumed the free-ride was on the *shared crop sink*, so an
*exclusive* animal sink would be safe. That assumption is **falsified**. The head-to-head
loss is driven by **opportunity cost, not sink-sharing**: buying land ($1000) + animals
($400–500 each) + a 3rd rancher (fib hire cost) diverts labor and cash away from the
shared high-value MELON/STRAWBERRY crop sinks. The non-expanding champion — which needs
no MILK/WOOL market to profit — then captures more of *those crop* markets. Both farms
end rich (cand ~59k, champ ~58–61k), but the champion's crop free-ride outweighs the
candidate's extra exclusive-sink revenue on ~11/15 seeds. The tragedy-of-commons is on
the **crops the expander under-serves**, not on the animal sink it adds. Combined with
SOT-2344's finding that MILK/WOOL are already near-saturated by the 6 NW animals, the
marginal exclusive revenue from NE animals cannot cover the land + labor + cross-board
travel cost head-to-head.

## Decision

**NON-PROMOTED.** `main.py` unchanged (champion SOT-2344 COW3/SHEEP3, NW-only, retained).
`cand_land.py` reverted (removed). No Kaggle submission (parent SOT-2285 owns the slot).

Land expansion is now rejected for **both** shared (SOT-2299) and exclusive (SOT-2343)
sinks: the binding constraint is the head-to-head opportunity cost of diverting from the
shared crop portfolio, which any expansion incurs. Future cycles should not retry land
expansion without a fundamentally different lever (e.g. an expansion that does NOT reduce
crop throughput, or a mechanic that denies the opponent the shared crop free-ride).
