## Goal (from the Issue)
SOT-2379 empirically proved self-mirror oracle drift: soil `00e8ab59` beat moon-198 **20/20** in the
local self-mirror oracle, yet on the real LB soil floored to **600** (relative-scoring noise floor) while

asks: **calibrate that league so it reproduces the known real-LB ordering `moon-198 ≫ soil`, then define a
trustworthy field promotion gate** for cycle-3 candidate selection (children SOT-2418 moon-449 / SOT-2419
kaitofukami-v17). The Issue explicitly allows the contingency: *if the league cannot be made to reproduce
the ordering, conclude "the league is also unreliable" and record the rationale.*

## Method
New calibration mode `scripts/eval_league.py --calibrate --subjects "path:label:real_lb,…"`:
- Each subject is scored vs every pool opponent whose **bytes differ** from it (self-play guard, so the
  soil anchor — which is also a pool file — never mirror-matches itself).
- **Real LB is a *field* ranking (each agent vs a diverse field), NOT a head-to-head between two agents.**
  So the calibration metric is **field win-rate**, compared on the **common opponent set** two subjects
  share (apples-to-apples). Calibration PASSES iff the league's field ordering is monotone with real LB
  for every LB-known pair.
- Subjects: `moon198` (`main.py`, LB 2543.4), `soil` (`opponents/soil_remembers_rain.py`, LB 600),
  `moon449` (`opponents/moon_counts_melons.py`, 449-line updated frontier, LB unknown).
- Seed split = SOT-2383 continuity: SCREEN `[101,202]` + CONFIRM `[7,42,303,404,505,777,1234,2026,5555,9001]`
  (12 seeds × both seats = 24 games / opponent).

## Diagnosis — the frontier league is structurally anti-correlated with the real LB (for this lineage)
The real "kaggriculture" LB is **relative-percentile scoring against the broad distribution of ~thousands
of mostly-weak/median public submissions**. moon-198 plays a **robust, high-money-floor, rarely-busting**
strategy: it out-earns the broad weak field reliably (→ LB 2543.4) but is out-competed head-to-head by
aggressive **capture-or-bust** frontier tapes that starve it to 0. soil is such a capture-or-bust tape: it
wins its frontier head-to-heads (and crushes weak roman) but on the real broad LB its fragility surfaces
and it floors to the 600 noise band.

A 5-opponent **all-frontier** league measures head-to-head market capture, which is **orthogonal —
indeed inverted — to broad-field relative robustness.** Two independent facts pin this down:
1. **No subset of the current pool reproduces `moon-198 ≫ soil`.** Even restricting to the weakest opponent
   (roman) ranks soil (100%) above moon-198 (tie). The distinct-architecture subset `{kaito,pilk,roman}`
   gives soil 81.9% vs moon-198 well below.
2. **Extending with more weak/broad baselines cannot fix it.** Both strong tapes crush weak opponents
   equally (both 100% vs pilkwang; both would ≈100% vs a trivial PASS agent), so weak additions do not
   separate them in moon-198's favor. The real-LB discriminator is *soil's specific fragility against the
   actual submitted distribution* — not reproducible offline from 5 frontier tapes. Reproducing it would
   require a faithful sample of the real broad field, which we do not have.

Per the Issue's explicit contingency, the conclusion is: **the replay-league field oracle is NOT
trustworthy for champion promotion — it is anti-correlated with real LB for the moon/soil lineage.**

## Calibrated promotion gate (redefined deliverable)
Because the league inverts real LB, **field win-rate is DEMOTED from a promotion gate to a NON-GATING
diagnostic.** The calibrated gate for cycle-3 champion selection is:

1. **Real-LB confirmation is the ONLY trustworthy promotion gate.** A candidate may replace the
   champion-of-record (`main.py`, moon-198, LB 2543.4) **only after a real Kaggle submission** (owned by
   the **parent SOT-2412 resume run**, one lineage slot) scores **≥ 2543.4**. Local self-mirror A/B (drift
   proven, SOT-2379) and frontier-league field% (inversion proven here) may **pre-screen / diversify**
   candidates but **must never promote on their own.**
2. **Frontier-league field% is advisory only.** A high league field% (e.g. moon-449's 93.8%) flags
   head-to-head frontier strength, **not** real-LB rank, and by itself is **not** evidence for promotion —
   it is the *same* market-capture artifact that inflates soil to 64.6% and busts the LB-champion moon-198
   to 27.5%.
3. **Guard:** never trust a single field oracle that disagrees with a known real-LB anchor. Both oracles we
   have (self-mirror, frontier-league) now have a documented anti-correlation with real LB on this lineage.
