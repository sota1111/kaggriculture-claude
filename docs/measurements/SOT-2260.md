# SOT-2260 — High-revenue crop portfolio + glut-aware selling

## Change

Keep the multi-worker routing engine (SOT-2259 farmer patrol + SOT-2261 five hired
hands) but replace the **single WHEAT crop** with a **diversified high-value crop
portfolio** and sell **glut-aware, highest-price-first**.

- Tile allocation (25 NW slots): `MELON 10 / STRAWBERRY 8 / WHEAT 7`.
- Selling: every turn, sell each shed product ordered by its current visible unit
  price (`obs["market"]["prices"]`) descending, within the 10-order budget, so
  scarce high-value produce clears before cheap wheat.
- Generic crop lifecycle handling: per-crop yield windows, ongoing crops
  (TOMATO/STRAWBERRY: harvest whenever `yield_units>0`), non-ongoing (WHEAT/MELON:
  harvest at `max_yield_day`), water any live un-watered plant (free; keeps it
  alive — 2 dry days kill a plant — and adds yield in-window).

## Why (market economics, read from the env)

The env prices each product `price(inv) = base ± amp·f(|inv - I0|)`, `I0 = 10000`,
plus a town-center demand schedule (drains **all** products every 12 steps, mult
1→2→4 by game third) and per-shop demand (every 4 steps for each unlocked shop's
products). Any product **nobody supplies** is drained below `I0` and priced at a
**scarcity premium**.

End-of-game market under all-WHEAT mirror play (seed 1):

| product | base | glut curve | price | inv (I0=10000) | in shops |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| WHEAT | 25 | log (shallow) | **20** | 10294 (glutted) | 5 shops + center |
| CARROT | 35 | sqrt | 42 | 9482 (scarce) | PET_CAFE, FARMERS_MARKET |
| TOMATO | 60 | sqrt | 105 | 9626 (scarce) | PIZZA, FARMERS_MARKET |
| STRAWBERRY | 120 | linear | **321** | 9428 (scarce) | 4 shops |
| MELON | 250 | sq | **293** | 9860 (scarce) | **0 shops** (center only) |

All-WHEAT dumps into the one already-saturated market (~$20) while the
$105/$321/$293 markets sit unfilled. Diverting tiles to those crops sells into the
unmet demand sinks. Over-planting one crop gluts it (MELON's `sq` glut collapses to
the $1 floor past its ~140-unit town-center sink), so tiles are **diversified**:
MELON for top value-density under its small sink, STRAWBERRY for its large 4-shop
sink, WHEAT for fast (first yield day 2) early cashflow while melon/strawberry
mature (first yield day 10-12).

## Environment / oracle

`kaggle_environments` `kaggriculture` (boardSize 10, turnsPerDay 24, episodeSteps
720 → 30 days, startingMoney 3000, shedCapacity 100, farmHandCostMult 1). Score =
final money. Harness `scripts/eval.py`: `env.run([A, B])`, A = player 0.

Two measures are reported. **vs champion** (SOT-2261 all-wheat engine) is the
promotion gate. **self-mirror** (both farmers flood the same portfolio) is the
honest glut stress test — the opponent competes for the same high-value markets, so
prices fall the most; it is the realistic floor if rivals copy the strategy.
