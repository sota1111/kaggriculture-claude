# SOT-2298 — Ration sells to the demand drains; avoid market glut (PROMOTED)

## Axis / hypothesis

The champion (`main.py`, SOT-2260) sells the **entire** shed every turn. But the env's
`SELL` raises `market["inventory"]` by +1 per unit (when price > 1), and
`price(inv) = base ± amp·f(|inv − I0|)`, `I0 = 10000`. So dumping a whole harvest burst
pushes inventory above `I0` and each successive unit clears cheaper. **MELON** is the
pinch point: `above_func = sq`, `above_target = 3.60` ⇒
`price = 250 − 0.01·(inv − I0)²`, which falls to ~$150 by `I0+100` and to the $1 floor by
`I0+158`. Demand is a small, scheduled drain — town **center** every 12 steps (mult
1 → 2 (day ≥ 10) → **4** (day ≥ 20)) plus per-shop pulls every 4 steps — so all-dump play
depresses its own price.

**Hypothesis:** meter the per-turn sell quantity of each product so its marginal unit
price stays at/above a scarcity threshold (keeping inventory near/under `I0`), holding the
rest in the shed for a later turn after the drains reopen headroom, then liquidate
everything on the final day (unsold shed value = $0 at game end). Production
(tiles/crops/hands) is unchanged — **market side only**.

## Change (`main.py`)

- `_meter_sell_qty(item, qty, inv0, day)`: sell only the units whose predicted marginal
  price `price(inv0 + j)` stays `≥ SELL_FLOOR_FRAC · base`; hold the remainder. On
  `day ≥ LIQUIDATE_DAY` dump the whole shed.
- `_market_price` / `_shape` mirror the env's `MARKET_PARAMS` to *predict* the marginal
  price (the env stays the source of truth; the prediction only sizes the order).
- Params (swept, below): `SELL_FLOOR_FRAC = 0.85`, `LIQUIDATE_DAY = 29` (the last game day;
  `episodeSteps 720 / turnsPerDay 24 → days 0..29`).
- High-value-first ordering and the `maxMarketOrdersPerTurn = 10` budget are unchanged.
- Only `import math` added (exec-harness safe; verified below).
